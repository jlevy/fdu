Linux scouting spike (research-2026-08-13-linux-first-measurements.md): building the fdu binary with mimalloc as global allocator improved the warm transient-summary plan 30.3% wall [-32.9%, -25.6%] at 450k entries (344->242 ms, CPU 1.23->0.94 s), enough to move fdu-summary decisively ahead of diskus on that rig. Full-index path unchanged (-0.9%, interval spans zero) - its cost is BTreeMap traversal, not allocation. Producers allocate paths/batches, the consumer frees them: the cross-thread-free pattern mimalloc is built for.
