Medium. crates/fdu-core/src/opened.rs DiscoveryFrontier::pop scanned the whole pending deque against every priority, then removed from the middle of a VecDeque: O(width x priorities) per pop, O(w^2 p) per breadth-first level. Reviewer measured in debug: no priorities 4,000 pops 167 ms vs 16,000 pops 3.41 s; 64 priorities 1,000 pops 2.19 s vs 4,000 pops 40.0 s. Fix: group pending directories by first matching priority when queued or when priorities change; pop from the first non-empty group, else the front. Related: fdu-sgp7.
