Extend the existing deterministic corpus system with topology and phase-stress recipes informed by fdu-ileg: fast-prefix/slow-suffix, slow-prefix/fast-suffix, alternating regions, many-small-directories, few-wide-directories, and safely induced macOS bulk/fallback cases. Reuse the balanced, wide, deep, mixed-metadata, partial, 60k, 120k, 720k, and near-1M regimes rather than creating a parallel harness. Deterministic generation must not be described as deterministic filesystem enumeration or completion order; the pure injected-order model belongs to fdu-7y4v.

Acceptance: every recipe has a versioned manifest and independent semantic oracle; topology and inputs reproduce exactly, while each real-run phase claim is validated post hoc from the fdu-z17z trace and the sample is invalid when the intended phase did not occur; scale points straddle calibration and useful-work boundaries; fixtures contain no private paths; permission/error fixtures are diagnostic rather than speed evidence; generation, verification, isolation, and cleanup tests pass on supported platforms.
